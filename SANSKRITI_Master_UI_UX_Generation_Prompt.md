# 0. MASTER INSTRUCTION TO THE UI GENERATION AGENT

You are the **lead product designer, UX architect, visual designer,
design-system engineer, and frontend experience strategist** for
**SANSKRITI**.

Your task is to design a complete, cohesive, production-quality
responsive web application for SANSKRITI.

Do not generate isolated screens that merely look attractive.

Design the entire product as **one connected system** with:

-   a clear information architecture
-   consistent navigation
-   a reusable component system
-   consistent spacing and alignment
-   a unified visual language
-   scalable data patterns
-   responsive desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts
-   realistic content and states
-   logical user journeys
-   strong cultural storytelling
-   clear booking conversion flows

The final product must feel like a serious, premium global startup, not
a concept-only Dribbble design.

The design must directly reflect the core SANSKRITI product idea:

> **SANSKRITI helps Indian and international travelers experience real
> Indian culture through authentic experiences hosted by verified local
> Cultural Ambassadors.**

SANSKRITI is not primarily a sightseeing platform.

It is not a hotel booking platform.

It is not a generic tour marketplace.

It is a platform for discovering and participating in **living Indian
culture**.

The product experience should communicate:

**Discover culture. Meet people. Participate for real.**

# 1. CORE PRODUCT UNDERSTANDING

SANSKRITI connects travelers and cultural learners with verified local
people who can share authentic cultural knowledge and experiences.

The core experiences may include:

-   Indian festivals
-   local family experiences
-   traditional food and cooking
-   crafts and artisan workshops
-   traditional music
-   dance
-   rituals and cultural traditions
-   regional heritage
-   local community experiences
-   cultural storytelling
-   live online cultural classes

The platform has two connected dimensions.

## A. PHYSICAL CULTURAL EXPERIENCES

Users travel to a location and participate in an authentic experience.

Examples:

-   celebrating Holi with a verified local family in Jaipur
-   learning traditional Rajasthani cooking
-   joining a textile or craft workshop
-   participating in a regional festival
-   learning a local dance directly from practitioners
-   spending time with an artisan community

## B. ONLINE CULTURAL LEARNING

Users can experience and learn about Indian culture before traveling or
without traveling.

Examples:

-   live cooking sessions
-   Indian language basics
-   classical dance introductions
-   festival rituals and cultural context
-   textile and craft learning
-   cultural storytelling sessions

The online experience is not a disconnected side feature.

It should visually and strategically connect to travel.

The ideal product loop is:

**DISCOVER CULTURE** ↓ **LEARN ONLINE** ↓ **BUILD INTEREST AND TRUST** ↓
**MEET CULTURAL AMBASSADORS** ↓ **BOOK AN IN-PERSON EXPERIENCE** ↓
**PARTICIPATE IN REAL CULTURE** ↓ **SHARE THE STORY** ↓ **RETURN TO
DISCOVER MORE**

# 3. VISUAL IDENTITY --- NON-NEGOTIABLE

## PRIMARY COLOR: WHITE

White is the dominant and primary visual foundation.

Use white generously.

The interface should feel:

-   clean
-   premium
-   open
-   calm
-   contemporary
-   globally accessible

White space is an active part of the design.

Do not fill every area with color.

Do not create dark backgrounds across the entire product.

The overall visual system should be predominantly white.

Recommended conceptual usage:

-   Main page background: pure or near-pure white
-   Cards: white or subtle off-white only when needed
-   Dividers: very light neutral tones
-   Large photography: used as visual moments
-   Dark text: high contrast
-   Yellow: controlled accent and interaction color

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 4. SECONDARY COLOR: YELLOW

Yellow is the signature secondary accent color.

The yellow should feel:

-   warm
-   optimistic
-   energetic
-   culturally expressive
-   premium

Avoid neon yellow.

Avoid overusing yellow as a full-page background.

Yellow should be used strategically for:

-   primary calls to action
-   active states
-   key highlights
-   selected dates
-   small visual accents
-   important numbers
-   hover details
-   progress indicators
-   cultural discovery markers

Suggested direction:

**Warm Saffron Gold / Deep Premium Yellow**

The exact shade should have enough depth to work with black typography
and white surfaces.

Example conceptual palette:

-   Primary background: #FFFFFF
-   Soft surface: #F7F7F4
-   Primary text: #151515
-   Secondary text: #6F6F6F
-   Yellow accent: approximately #F4C430 to #F6C445 range
-   Light yellow surface: subtle tinted yellow for hover or highlighted
    backgrounds
-   Border: #E8E8E8

Do not use random additional bright colors.

Photography should provide the broader cultural color diversity.

# 8. GLOBAL GRID AND ALIGNMENT SYSTEM

This is critical.

Every page must feel structurally related.

Do not independently position elements based only on visual convenience.

Create one global layout system.

## DESKTOP

Use a 12-column grid.

Recommended principles:

-   large centered max-width content container
-   consistent outer margins
-   consistent column gutters
-   predictable vertical spacing
-   repeated alignment anchors

Suggested maximum content width:

**1440px to 1520px maximum**

Core content can use a slightly narrower readable width where
appropriate.

Every major section should align to the same global content edges unless
deliberately full bleed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## TABLET

Do not simply shrink the desktop.

Reflow:

-   grids
-   cards
-   image ratios
-   navigation
-   sticky panels

Use fewer columns while preserving hierarchy.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## MOBILE

Mobile is a separate layout decision.

Do not stack desktop screens blindly.

Prioritize information according to the user's immediate task.

For example, on an experience page:

1.  Experience image
2.  Experience title
3.  Date and location
4.  Cultural Ambassador
5.  Key information
6.  Booking CTA

Use a mobile-first information hierarchy.

-   reflow grids

## MOBILE

-   one primary task per screen area
-   large touch targets
-   clear hierarchy
-   sticky booking CTA where relevant
-   full-screen filters
-   carefully cropped imagery

Do not allow:

-   overlapping text
-   broken image crops
-   horizontal overflow
-   tiny filter controls
-   excessive compressed content

# 31. MICROINTERACTIONS

Use subtle interaction.

Examples:

-   image reveal on hover
-   card elevation or slight scale
-   active festival transition
-   smooth filter changes
-   saved experience state
-   button press feedback

Do not use excessive animations.

Motion should support:

-   discovery
-   hierarchy
-   feedback

# 32. ACCESSIBILITY

Ensure:

-   readable text contrast
-   visible keyboard focus
-   large touch targets
-   meaningful labels
-   accessible forms
-   color-independent states
-   clear error messages
-   descriptive image alternatives where required

# 33. DATA AND CONTENT REALISM

Use realistic sample data.

Do not use placeholder text such as:

Lorem Ipsum.

Do not use repetitive fake names.

Content should feel grounded in the SANSKRITI concept.

Use varied locations and cultural categories.

However, do not imply that a particular person, family, or experience is
real unless verified by actual product data.

Sample content must clearly function as representative interface
content.

# 34. PRODUCT SCALABILITY

The initial business strategy is to start small:

**One festival + one city**

Example:

Holi in Jaipur.

The UI must work beautifully with:

-   5 experiences
-   10 Cultural Ambassadors
-   one major festival

Do not design the initial experience as if the marketplace already has
millions of listings.

The system should then scale to:

## Stage 1

One festival, one city.

## Stage 2

Multiple festivals across one or two states.

## Stage 3

Major festivals across India.

## Stage 4

International traveler reach and partnerships.

The UI architecture must scale without a complete redesign.

Platform commission-based.

## FESTIVAL JOURNEYS

Multiple experiences combined into a package.

Future integration with:

-   stays
-   travel partners

-   transportation partners

# 36. EMPTY STATES

Create thoughtful empty states.

Examples:

No experiences found.

No saved experiences.

No upcoming classes.

No search results.

Do not use emojis.

Use:

-   minimal illustration
-   clear headline
-   helpful explanation
-   useful CTA

# 37. LOADING STATES

Use:

-   skeleton layouts
-   image placeholders
-   subtle progress feedback

# 38. ERROR STATES

-   clear
-   calm
-   actionable

TRY AGAIN

Do not blame the user.
